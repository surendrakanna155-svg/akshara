NIKSHA OS — Parent & User Terms

Document version: 1.0
Status: Draft for owner sign-off (see PLACEHOLDERS.md)
Operator: NIKSHA Technologies Pvt. Ltd. ("NIKSHA OS", "we", "us").

These are the plain-language terms for individual users of the NIKSHA OS app — parents/guardians, teachers, staff and students. They sit under the Terms & Conditions and the Acceptable Use Policy. Your school is the organisation that runs NIKSHA OS for you.

1. Your account comes from your school

  • Your school created your access. You log in with your mobile number and a one-time password (OTP).
  • You can only see what your role allows. A parent sees only their own child(ren); a teacher sees only their classes.
  • Keep your login private. Don't share your OTP. If you lose your phone or think someone else has access, tell your school and email [SECURITY EMAIL] so we can secure your account.

2. What you can use the app for

Use NIKSHA OS for genuine school purposes — checking attendance, results, fees, timetable, notices, communicating with the school, and the features your role provides. Please follow the Acceptable Use Policy. Misuse can lead to your access being suspended.

3. Your child's information (for parents)

  • The information about your child is held by NIKSHA OS on behalf of your school. Your school decides what is recorded and is the first point of contact for questions, corrections, or removal.
  • You can review your child's information in the app and ask the school to correct anything that's wrong.
  • We do not use your child's data for advertising, tracking, or profiling. See Children's Data & Consent.

4. Notifications

With your permission, we send push and in-app notifications about school events (results published, fee due, attendance, notices). You can turn these off in your device or app settings at any time. We don't send marketing spam.

5. Payments (if your school enables online fees)

  • If you pay fees in the app, the payment is handled by a secure payment provider (see Sub-processors). NIKSHA OS does not store your full card details.
  • Your school sets the fees, due dates and refund rules. Questions or disputes about a fee, a charge, or a refund go to your school, not to NIKSHA OS.

6. AI features

Some features use AI to summarise information or draft text (for example, parent insights). AI can be wrong. Treat AI output as a helpful summary, not an official record — the school's records and staff are the authority. See the AI Usage & Disclaimer.

7. Content you upload

If you upload photos, documents or messages, make sure you have the right to share them and that they follow the Acceptable Use Policy. We may remove content that breaks the rules.

8. Your privacy & rights

Our Privacy Policy explains what we process and why. You can:

  • access and correct your data (usually via your school),
  • ask for erasure when data is no longer needed,
  • withdraw consent where processing relies on consent,
  • nominate someone to act for you (as the law allows), and
  • raise a grievance.

Because your school is usually the Data Fiduciary, start with your school. You can also contact our Grievance Officer at [GRIEVANCE EMAIL], and, if unresolved, the
Data Protection Board of India.

9. Accepting these terms

On first login — and whenever we make a material update — you may be asked to
review and accept these terms and the Privacy Policy before you continue. We record that you accepted (which version, when, and from which device) to keep a clear record. You can view the current policies any time from the app's Profile / Legal section.

  • To the extent the law allows, NIKSHA OS isn't liable for indirect losses, and our responsibility is limited as set out in the Terms & Conditions §12. Nothing here removes rights you have under law that can't be removed.
  • These terms are governed by Indian law.
