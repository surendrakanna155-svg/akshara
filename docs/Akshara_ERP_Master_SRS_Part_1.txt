1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Product Name:
Akshara ERP

Product Category:
Cloud-Based Multi-Tenant School ERP SaaS Platform

Product Vision:
Akshara ERP aims to become a unified digital ecosystem for educational institutions by connecting school management, principals, teachers, parents, students, finance departments, hostel operations, transport operations, marketing teams, and the Akshara ERP company itself through a single secure platform.

The system will eliminate manual processes, improve communication, automate repetitive tasks using Artificial Intelligence, and provide real-time insights for better decision making.
