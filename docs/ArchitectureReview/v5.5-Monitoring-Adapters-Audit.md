## Recommendations

1. Replace `NoOpVendorMonitoringTransport` with SDK-backed transports after Sentry/Datadog accounts are provisioned.
2. Keep vendor credentials in deployment configuration only; do not commit DSNs or tokens.
3. Prioritize v5.6 and v5.7 only when backend RBAC/RLS and audit ingestion endpoints are available.

## Verdict

**PASS** — v5.5 completes the client-side vendor adapter layer while preserving the existing monitoring architecture.
