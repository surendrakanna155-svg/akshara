# v7.5 Smart Timetable + Workload Engine — Architecture Review

**Score:** 90/100 · **Status:** Complete

## Summary

v7.5 adds a catalog-driven academic timetable engine on top of `teacher_assignments`, `classes`, and `sections`. Draft timetables are generated per section, validated for teacher/section/room conflicts and period gaps, then published with RBAC separation between manage and publish. AI Copilot academic assistant receives read-only timetable context and scheduling recommendations — no autonomous mutations.
