# Principal Guide — Akshara School OS

## Daily workflow

1. **Morning** — Open Principal Intelligence Center for school health score, at-risk students, and overnight alerts
2. **Mid-day** — Review attendance anomalies and communication delivery stats
3. **Afternoon** — Check fee collection summary and intervention recommendations

## Weekly workflow

- Student success dashboard — dropout and attendance risk trends
- Teacher effectiveness — syllabus coverage and lesson analytics
- Exam analytics — class-wise performance after assessments
- Communication analytics — parent engagement and campaign effectiveness

## Monthly workflow

- Academic progress report (principal view)
- Finance executive dashboard (read-only unless finance admin)
- Inventory reconciliation with finance
- Promotion / achievement preview (if term-end)

## Key screens

| Task | Navigation |
|------|------------|
| School health | Intelligence → Principal Center |
| At-risk students | Intelligence → Student Success |
| Staff performance | Intelligence → Teacher Effectiveness |
| Communications | School → Communication Analytics |
| Timetable issues | School Completion → Timetable Intelligence |

## Permissions

Principal role typically has `viewAnalytics`, `viewSis`, and read access to finance summaries. Fee collection and inventory writes remain with accountant / inventory manager.
