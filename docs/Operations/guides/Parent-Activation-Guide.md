# Parent Activation Guide

**Version:** 1.0 (v1.0-rc1)  
**For:** Parents and guardians of enrolled students

---

## 1. How your account is created

When the school imports student data, your **name** and **mobile number** are taken from the student record (`parentName`, `parentPhone`).

- You do **not** need a separate signup form.
- The same phone number for **two children** (siblings) → one login, both children visible.
- A **second guardian** (e.g. father if mother was on the sheet) receives a **WhatsApp invite link** from the school — tap the link, then log in with that phone.

---

## 2. First login (Parent app)

1. Install/open the **NIKSHA OS** app (link from school).
2. Enter your **mobile number** (10 digits, as registered with the school).
3. Tap **Send OTP** — enter the code from **SMS**.
4. Select scope **Parent** and your **school name**.
5. Your child’s dashboard appears (attendance, fees, notices, etc.).

**Important:** Choose **Parent**, not Student or School.

---

## 3. Student app (only if school enabled it)

Some schools also enable the **Student app** for older students. That requires a separate **student phone** on the import sheet.

- Student login uses **Student ID** (= admission number, e.g. `SCH-2026-0042`) + OTP to the student’s phone.
- If the school did not add a student phone, use the **Parent app** to view the child’s information.

---

## 5. Common issues

| Problem | What to try |
|---------|-------------|
| OTP not received | Wait 60 s; retry; confirm number matches school records |
| “No children linked” | Ask school to verify your phone on the student import row |
| Wrong school shown | Log out; log in again and pick correct school |
| Two children, one missing | School may have used different phones per child — ask office to align |

---

## 6. Privacy & security

- Do not share your OTP with anyone.
- Log out on shared devices.
- Contact the school office for phone number changes — updates require admin action.
