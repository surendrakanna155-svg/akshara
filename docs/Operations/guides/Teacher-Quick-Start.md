# Teacher Quick-Start Guide

**Version:** 1.0 (v1.0-rc1)  
**For:** Teachers and principals using Akshara Teacher app / web

---

## 1. Before you start

Your school admin must import your row in the **teacher CSV** with your correct mobile number. You cannot self-register until that import is committed.

---

## 2. First login

1. Open the **Teacher** app (or teacher web URL from your school).
2. Enter your **mobile number** exactly as the school registered it.
3. Tap **Send OTP** — check SMS for the 6-digit code.
4. Enter OTP → select **School** scope → your school name.
5. You should see the teacher dashboard.

**Principal?** Same steps — your import row used `role=principal`.

---

## 3. Daily essentials

| Task | What to do |
|------|------------|
| **Attendance** | Open Attendance → select your class/section → mark present/absent → **Submit** |
| **Timetable** | Timetable → view today’s periods (after school publishes timetable) |
| **Messages** | Messages → contact parents of your class (if enabled) |
| **Notices** | Read school broadcasts in Notifications |

Submit attendance **before end of school day** unless your admin says otherwise.

---

## 4. Tips

- Use the same phone number the school imported — a different number will not work.
- If OTP does not arrive, wait 60 seconds and retry; check network/SMS blocking.
- You cannot see other schools’ data — only your school.
- Finance collection and fee edits are usually **admin/accountant** tasks, not teacher tasks.

---

## 5. Need help?

Contact your school office first. They can verify your number is on the teacher import list and ask Akshara support if needed.
