## 1. Executive summary — what changed from the June roadmap and why

**June roadmap:** vision-led — AI School Builder / Organization Builder / Dynamic
Widget Platform / Verticals were treated as the *immediate* next builds.

**What the audits changed:** the moat features — **AI Copilot, Parent Insights,
WhatsApp, Communications, Memories, Branding, Broadcasts, Approvals, Admissions
funnel, Enrollment conversion, Parent engagement — are already LIVE and reusable**,
and the two revenue engines are nearly done (**Marketing ≈ 70%, Admissions CRM ≈
65–70%**). So the *first* work should finish near-done revenue features and harvest
the already-live AI — the highest ROI per week of effort.

**What did NOT change:** the long-term vision. The four platform pillars stay on the
roadmap as the differentiation + expansion moat. **AI School Builder is promoted to
P2 (Phase 1 starts within this horizon)**; Organization Builder and Dynamic Widgets
sit at P3 (platform expansion); Verticals at P4 (long-term expansion). They are
sequenced *after* the revenue layer, not removed from it.

**Net change vs June:** *"build the platform pillars now"* → *"finish the 2 revenue
engines + harvest live AI first (P1); begin the moat (AI School Builder Phase 1,
Director, AI Predictions) at P2; expand the platform (Org Builder, Dynamic Widgets)
at P3; long-term verticals at P4."* Same destination, ROI-ordered path.

## 3. Re-ranking rationale (A pilot-value · B revenue · C differentiation · D effort)

- **A. Pilot value:** a pilot must *convert the inquiries it already has* and *keep
  parents engaged* → Admissions CRM, Parent Insights, AI Admissions Assistant (P1).
- **B. Revenue:** first cheques ride on enrolment outcomes + tiered packaging →
  Admissions CRM, Marketing Engine, Capability Gating (P1).
- **C. Differentiation:** the moat splits in two — *already-shipped* AI/WhatsApp/Comms
  (surface & polish, cheap) **and** the *vision pillars* (AI School Builder, Director,
  AI Predictions at P2; Org Builder, Dynamic Widgets at P3; Verticals at P4). The
  vision is the long-game moat; it is funded and de-risked by the P1 revenue layer.
- **D. Effort:** P1 items are 60–85% done (days–weeks); the pillars are months. Doing
  revenue first buys the runway (and the pilot evidence) to build the pillars well.

## 6. Sequencing decisions (explicit answers)

**Q1. Admissions CRM before Marketing Engine?** → **Yes, CRM first.** Both ~70% and share
the live WhatsApp/Comms/Broadcast plumbing, but CRM is the **revenue/conversion end** — a
pilot must convert the inquiries it already has before mass lead-gen pays off. Build CRM's
follow-up/conversion loop first, then the Marketing MVP that feeds it (overlap on shared bits).

**Q2. Marketing Engine MVP before AI School Builder?** → **Yes — Marketing MVP (P1) first;
AI School Builder Phase 1 follows at P2.** This is sequencing, not deprioritizing: the 70%-done
revenue engine lands in weeks and funds the Builder; the Builder then starts as an explicit P2
differentiation bet (Phase 1 on the certified onboarding foundation).

**Q3. Keep Dynamic Widgets + Organization Builder deferred?** → **Sequenced to P3, not dropped.**
Both are 5–10% complete, multi-month, and backend-less today (Org Builder is OFF in
`config/live_release.json` because it would 404). They remain the platform-expansion moat,
built after the P1 revenue layer + P2 differentiation are in market.

**Q4. Verticals frozen until pilot validation?** → **Held at P4, not cancelled.** Verticals are
a long-term TAM expansion; unfreeze once the pilot proves retention and the first paying schools
indicate which vertical earns the investment.
