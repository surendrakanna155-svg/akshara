# v9.4 — Homework Intelligence Bridge

**Tag:** `v9.4-homework-intelligence-bridge`  
**Date:** June 2026

## Summary

Connects Question Bank, Question Papers, Homework Generator, Student Risk Engine, and Teacher Success Center. Teachers select class, subject, and exam type to receive weak topics, risk students, revision/worksheet suggestions, and recommended homework with optional auto-apply to `edu_homework_assignments`.

## Deliverables

- Tables: `homework_intelligence_runs`, `edu_homework_student_targets`
- API: `GET /intelligence/homework-intelligence/plan`, `POST /intelligence/homework-intelligence/generate`
- Flutter: `/homework-intelligence` screen
- Permissions: `viewHomeworkIntelligence`, `manageHomeworkIntelligence`
- Audit/domain: `intelligence.homework.generated`
