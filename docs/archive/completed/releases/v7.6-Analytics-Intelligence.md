# v7.6 — Analytics & Intelligence

**Status:** Complete

## Scope

Read-only analytics foundation, school health engine, intelligence APIs, Copilot extensions, and management UI for risk, trends, and executive summaries.

## Delivered

| Area | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Analytics engine | Eight metric domains: student/attendance/academic/fee risk, admissions conversion, teacher workload, timetable health, communication engagement |
| School health | Weighted composition across academic, finance, operations, engagement (25% each) |
| APIs | Dashboard, trends, risks, health, recommendations, principal summary, weekly briefing |
| Copilot | Academic assistant context bundle with analytics; explain risk/health/trends/anomalies/briefings |
| Flutter UI | Intelligence hub — analytics, school health, risk center, trend explorer, principal summary |
| Permissions | `viewAnalytics`, `viewSchoolHealth` |
