# v8.9 — Student Risk Prediction Engine

**Tag:** `v8.9-student-risk-prediction`  
**Date:** June 2026

## Summary

Computes student risk scores from attendance, marks, homework completion, communication gaps, behavior records, and timetable patterns. Produces low/medium/high/critical levels with reasons, interventions, teacher actions, and parent notification recommendations.

## Deliverables

- Table: `intel_student_risk_snapshots`
- API: `GET /intelligence/risk/students`, `GET /intelligence/risk/classes`, `POST /intelligence/risk/students/compute`
- Permissions: `viewStudentRisk`, `generateIntelligence`
- Flutter: Student Risk tab in `/intelligence`
- Audit: `studentRiskComputed`
