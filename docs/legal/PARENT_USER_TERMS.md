## 1. Your account comes from your school

- Your school created your access. You log in with your **mobile number and a
  one-time password (OTP)**.
- You can only see what your role allows. A **parent sees only their own child(ren)**;
  a **teacher sees only their classes**.
- Keep your login private. **Don't share your OTP.** If you lose your phone or think
  someone else has access, tell your school and email **[SECURITY EMAIL]** so we can
  secure your account.

## 2. What you can use the app for

Use Akshara for genuine school purposes — checking attendance, results, fees,
timetable, notices, communicating with the school, and the features your role
provides. Please follow the [Acceptable Use Policy](ACCEPTABLE_USE_POLICY.md). Misuse
can lead to your access being suspended.

## 3. Your child's information (for parents)

- The information about your child is held by Akshara **on behalf of your school**.
  Your **school** decides what is recorded and is the first point of contact for
  questions, corrections, or removal.
- You can **review your child's information** in the app and ask the school to
  correct anything that's wrong.
- We do **not** use your child's data for advertising, tracking, or profiling. See
  [Children's Data & Consent](CHILDREN_DATA_AND_CONSENT.md).

## 4. Notifications

With your permission, we send **push and in-app notifications** about school events
(results published, fee due, attendance, notices). You can turn these off in your
device or app settings at any time. We don't send marketing spam.

## 5. Payments (if your school enables online fees)

- If you pay fees in the app, the payment is handled by a secure **payment provider**
  (see [Sub-processors](SUBPROCESSORS.md)). Akshara does **not** store your full card
  details.
- **Your school sets the fees, due dates and refund rules.** Questions or disputes
  about a fee, a charge, or a refund go to your **school**, not to Akshara.

## 6. AI features

Some features use AI to summarise information or draft text (for example, parent
insights). **AI can be wrong.** Treat AI output as a helpful summary, not an official
record — the school's records and staff are the authority. See the
[AI Usage & Disclaimer](AI_USAGE_AND_DISCLAIMER.md).

## 7. Content you upload

If you upload photos, documents or messages, make sure you have the right to share
them and that they follow the [Acceptable Use Policy](ACCEPTABLE_USE_POLICY.md). We
may remove content that breaks the rules.

## 8. Your privacy & rights

Our [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md) explains what we process and why. You can:
- access and correct your data (usually via your school),
- ask for erasure when data is no longer needed,
- withdraw consent where processing relies on consent,
- nominate someone to act for you (as the law allows), and
- raise a grievance.

Because your **school is usually the Data Fiduciary**, start with your school. You can
also contact our Grievance Officer at **[GRIEVANCE EMAIL]**, and, if unresolved, the
**Data Protection Board of India**.

## 9. Accepting these terms

On first login — and whenever we make a material update — you may be asked to
**review and accept** these terms and the Privacy Policy before you continue. We
record that you accepted (which version, when, and from which device) to keep a clear
record. You can view the current policies any time from the app's **Profile / Legal**
section.
